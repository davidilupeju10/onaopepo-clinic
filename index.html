About

Healthcare close to home, built around people.

Accessible, affordable healthcare for individuals and families in Okinni, Osun State, and the communities around it.

Onaopepo Clinic & Maternity Center is dedicated to providing accessible and affordable healthcare to individuals and families in Okinni and surrounding communities.

Our focus is general medical care, maternal health and child wellbeing. We aim to give professional services in an environment built around dignity, respect and compassion.

Whether you come for a medical consultation, for care during pregnancy, or to bring your baby into the world, you are cared for as a person first.
