Privacy and terms

How this website handles your information, and a medical notice.

Privacy policy

This website does not store what you type into the booking form. When you press Send, your name, phone number, chosen service and message are placed into a WhatsApp message that you can review and send yourself. Once sent, WhatsApp's own policies apply. Links to WhatsApp, Google Maps and other external services are governed by those services. Your light or dark mode choice is saved only in your own browser.

Terms of use and medical notice

Information on this website is general and may change. It is not a medical diagnosis or personal medical advice. For clinical advice, service availability or opening arrangements, contact the clinic directly. In an emergency, call the clinic or go to the nearest health facility without delay. External websites are run independently and have their own terms.
